# Low-poly reference board (2026-10-02)

The reference board the engineer used to pick the visual direction (xperiaroco2/prime-game#165, comment "wave 1b"):
30 games in nine families and three asset packs, from three web research agents on 2026-10-02. The private page
showed Steam store screenshots; they were never committed and are not reproduced here. Translated from the Ukrainian
page. `[unconfirmed]` marks a claim with no primary source.

**Outcome:** the engineer chose How to Fish's characters as the direction, with five fingers for hand gestures and
clothing in separate pieces (see `docs/pipeline.md`).

Hands: "five fingers", "no fingers", "unknown" (not checked), "not visible". Clothing: "pieces" (separate parts),
"sets" (whole outfits only), "pieces and sets", "none".
